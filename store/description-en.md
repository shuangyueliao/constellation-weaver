# Constellation Weaver — Store Submission Materials

## Short description (CrazyGames / GameDistribution / itch.io, 196 chars)

Rotate the night sky and gather linked stars into 25 original constellations.
Mind the moonlight, dodge the dark stars, never cross your strands — then
WEAVE. A celestial spin on connection puzzles.

## Details

- Genre: Puzzle / Casual / Brain
- Controls: Mouse / Touch — drag to rotate the sky. Buttons: TILT I/II flips
  the axis, WEAVE validates. Keyboard: ←/→ rotate, T tilt, Enter/Space weave.
- Features: 25 handcrafted levels; rotate-to-configuration puzzles on a
  celestial sphere; moonlight, dark-star and strand-crossing constraints;
  five drifting-sky time trials; star ratings (moves + time); progress saved
  in localStorage; procedural graphics & sounds; single-file HTML5 (no
  external assets), works offline once loaded.
- Tech: Vanilla JavaScript, Canvas, WebAudio. No frameworks, no tracking.
